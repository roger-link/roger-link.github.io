What belongs here

Access List is for opportunities where a free entry, sign-up, or timed action gives people a fair shot to purchase scarce inventory at retail or face value, and the item has a real secondary market.

What does not

Paid-entry contests, casino-style gambling, prize-only giveaways, non-transferable race entries, personal-use event lotteries, unclear third-party raffles, and listings without an official source are out of scope. Known resale restrictions should be flagged instead of hidden.

Keep it useful

Listing checklist

  • Entry is free, or the cost is clearly disclosed.
  • The user is seeking access to purchase at retail or face value.
  • The item has an active resale market and is not tied to one named participant.
  • Known resale restrictions are flagged clearly.
  • The official source link is visible and trustworthy.
  • The access type is labeled without implying a prize or guaranteed purchase.
  • Region, eligibility, open date, close date, and price are easy to scan.

Access List is an independent directory concept. Always verify details with the official source.
